# Overview, inbox, contacts and analytics

## Overview

Show only real bounded measures: conversation/reply activity, active agents/channels, usage/quota and recent operational items. Empty workspaces receive clear setup actions; charts and trends are omitted when their data does not exist.

## Unified inbox

Desktop uses a filter/list pane and a conversation detail pane; mobile navigates list to detail with a back action. Filters include channel, agent, assignee, status, tag and period as implemented.

The thread displays normalized messages, provider/delivery state, validated attachments and product/source evidence. M1 adds reply, AI/human handoff, assignment, unread, status, notes and tags. Actions must show permission, conflict and retry feedback without losing composed text.

## Contacts and leads

Contacts show consent-aware identity, channel identities, conversations/leads timeline, notes, tags and assignee. Anonymous visitors are not given invented names. Identity linking/merge requires explicit evidence, confirmation and recoverability. CSV/export is produced by authorized backend endpoints, not reconstructed from rendered cells.

## Analytics

Metrics state their period/timezone and have accessible tabular equivalents. Channel volume, response time, AI/human resolution, lead conversion and product interest appear only after their events and definitions exist. No fake revenue uplift, provider cost or connection count.
